# Two visible copies of the same message are two messages; re-reading them gives none; a third copy gives one.
@frame
 10  28 | Bob: gg
 32  50 | Kim: wp
 54  72 | Bob: gg
@expect
chat | - | Bob | gg
chat | - | Kim | wp
chat | - | Bob | gg
@frame
 10  28 | Bob: gg
 32  50 | Kim: wp
 54  72 | Bob: gg
@expect
(none)
@frame
 10  28 | Kim: wp
 32  50 | Bob: gg
 54  72 | Bob: gg
 76  94 | Bob: gg
@expect
chat | - | Bob | gg
